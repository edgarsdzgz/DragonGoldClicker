THE WARDEN · HEAD STUDY V3: LEAN AND MUSCLED

48×48 grid, 16 colours, Ember palette. Flat colour clusters only: no texture pass and no dithering. Light from the upper front. Built from the rules in the dragon-art research: a lizard skull, a raptor brow over a small slit eye, ram horns rooted at the back of the skull, a thick neck.

Shadows shift toward violet, light toward orange, so no tone is plain black or plain white.

What changed from V2

  • No pouch: the neck no longer flares under the jaw. It is an even S-curve, 8-9 pixels wide, until it meets the shoulders.
  • Muscle: a lit cord runs from behind the jaw to the chest, with a shadow groove behind it. The shoulder has a lit top, a shadow side and a groove where it meets the chest.
  • Throat plates: narrowed to a strip down the front, so the neck reads as hide and muscle, not a gold bib.
  • Spines: four, set down the back of the neck, shrinking toward the shoulders. The grey frill behind the jaw is gone.
  • Kept: the head, eye, brow and horns from V2, and the same 16 colours.

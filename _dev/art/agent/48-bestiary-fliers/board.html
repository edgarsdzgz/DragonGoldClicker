The bestiary: fliers and riders

No new battlefield needed (owner): fliers use the road, drawn higher over a shadow of their own, and their two walk frames are a wingbeat (the Storm Rider's a gallop on the wind), so the game plays them where it plays any walk. Riders on mounts simply walk. Built like the locked bestiary: shaded, no blending, every pose at all three Unmaking tiers. Lifting fliers and drawing the shadow is app work. · 2026-10-27

Fliers

Giant Bat

A brown bat as big as a goblin: big ears, gold eyes, fangs, leathery wings.

Harpy

A gray-green hag's face under stringy black hair, feathered wings, yellow legs with talons.

Mantair Corsair

A raider in a rust head-wrap riding a flying manta: wide sea-dark wings, cephalic fins, a curved blade. From the old Draconia game.

Storm Rider

A Wind-Taken rider on a storm-gray horse that runs on the wind: a white blaze, a cloud mane, lightning under the hooves, a lance with a pennant. Its two frames are a gallop, not a wingbeat.

Riders

Goblin Wolf Rider

A goblin with a spear on a gray wolf with the War Hound's head, bigger. A ground rider: it walks the road like any monster.

Batch 6: People

The game's roles and the wanderers, at townsfolk scale and in the fighter's style · 2026-10-27

The roles beside the townsfolk (x6)

Feet on one line, as they will stand in the game. Each role is a different people, so the line-up shows the whole town.

Soldier ranks (x8)

Rank reads from gear alone: the conscript in an undyed, patched tunic with a rope belt and a dull spear; the footman in a nasal helmet and dark gambeson behind a round shield; the knight in plate under a gold tabard, a great helm with a gold plume, and a lance with a gold pennant.

Forager tiers (x8)

The gatherer is the halfling with a basket of greens and berries on the hip; the trapper is the orc in a fur cap with a pelt over the shoulder and a coil of snare rope at the belt; the ranger is an elf under a green hood with a longbow.

Explorer and repairer (x8)

The explorer is horned folk in a traveling coat with pack straps, a crook staff and a lantern. The repairer is the dwarf with a hammer: raised, then striking, 2 frames at 4 fps.

Wanderer groups (x6)

Small parties on a strip of road, for the arrival events: a lone traveler with a walking staff, a couple, a family with a child, and an old soldier on a crutch with an eye patch and a bandaged leg.

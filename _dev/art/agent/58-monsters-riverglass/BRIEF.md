# Brief: Riverglass Delta (levels 31-40, element steam)

Land: Marsh and river things scalded by the steaming delta. No faction in the lore yet; proposed: the Delta Drowned, creatures of the hot reed-water.

## The monsters

### 1. Kappa (regular)
- Origin: Japanese folklore, Japan
- Looks: Child-sized green scaly water imp with a turtle shell, beak and a water-filled dish on its head.
- Behavior: Older tales have it drowning people and livestock and pulling out the mythical shirikodama; spilling its dish weakens it.
- Type: swarm
- Reads at game size: yes: shell and dish silhouette
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kappa_(folklore) (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Sensitivity note: Widely used in fiction; some shrines honor kappa as water spirits, so keep it a pest, not a deity. Unverified, one source.
- Fitter's note: Swarm. Japan.

### 2. Kongamato (regular)
- Origin: Jiundu swamps, Zambia, Angola, Congo
- Looks: A winged creature with a 4 to 7 foot wingspan, reddish skin and a toothy beak.
- Behavior: Haunts rivers and capsizes boats from below.
- Type: flier
- Reads at game size: yes: red pterosaur-like flier
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kongamato (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Sensitivity note: Unverified, one source. Cryptid.
- Fitter's note: Flier: leathery-winged river raider. Zambia. Stymphalian Birds is rated event, not regular.

### 3. Nixie (regular)
- Origin: Germany and Scandinavia
- Looks: A small grey water-dweller with a large head, or a river woman or man; sometimes plays a golden harp or takes horse shape.
- Behavior: Steals unbaptized children and leaves its own strong, big-headed changelings; lures people to underwater homes. Tales warn of rivers and mill ponds.
- Type: ranged
- Reads at game size: yes: small gray water figure with big head
- Sources so far: https://sites.pitt.edu/~dash/gerchange.html (read 2026-10-29) | https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/41006/pg41006.txt (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Fitter's note: Ranged water-luring spirit. Germany.

### 4. Jenny Greenteeth (elite)
- Origin: England, Lancashire and the North (Peg Powler on the Tees)
- Looks: A green-skinned river hag with long hair, sharp teeth and long arms, hiding under pond weed.
- Behavior: Waits under duckweed and drags children and the careless into the water to drown them. Used as a warning to keep children from ponds.
- Type: brute
- Reads at game size: yes: green hag with hair over a water line
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenny_Greenteeth (read 2026-10-29) | https://sites.pitt.edu/~dash/bogey.html (read 2026-10-29) | https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/41148/pg41148.txt (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Fitter's note: Brute lurking in weed. England.

### 5. Ahuizotl (elite)
- Origin: Aztec (Nahua), Mexico
- Looks: A dog-sized water beast with black rubbery fur, monkey-like hands and a tail ending in a hand.
- Behavior: Lives in deep pools, snatches people from the shore and takes eyes, nails and teeth; mimics a baby's cry to lure victims.
- Type: brute
- Reads at game size: yes: tail-hand is a clear mark.
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahuizotl_(mythology) (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: yellow
- Sensitivity note: Tied to the rain gods; priests alone handled its dead. Many descendants keep Nahua tradition. Not worshipped itself. Unverified, one source.
- Fitter's note: Water-dog with a hand on its tail. Aztec, yellow (living belief).

### 6. Berberoka (mini-boss)
- Origin: Philippine folklore, northern Luzon (Apayao, Abra, Ilocos Norte)
- Looks: A swamp creature; the page gives no body description, so the design would be mine.
- Behavior: Drains a pond so the fish lie stranded, then floods it back over those who come to gather them.
- Type: brute
- Reads at game size: yes if drawn as a big swamp lump with a wide mouth
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berberoka (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Sensitivity note: unverified, one source (Wikipedia only); second source to be checked before art. The page says nothing about living belief.
- Fitter's note: Level 35. Drains the pond to catch prey. Philippines.

### 7. Lernaean Hydra (boss)
- Origin: Ancient Greek myth, Greece
- Looks: A huge nine-headed water serpent; the middle head is deathless.
- Behavior: Lives in the swamp at Lerna and raids flocks and fields; cut-off heads regrow doubled unless the stumps are burned.
- Type: brute
- Reads at game size: yes: multi-head silhouette.
- Sources so far: https://www.theoi.com/Ther/DrakonHydra.html (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Sensitivity note: Classical myth, widely used in fiction. Unverified, one source.
- Fitter's note: Level 40. Multi-headed marsh serpent. Greek.

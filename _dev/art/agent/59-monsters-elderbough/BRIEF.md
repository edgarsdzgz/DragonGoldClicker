# Brief: Elderbough Crown (levels 41-50, element mist)

Land: Feral fey of an ancient misty forest. Tricksters and wood-wardens gone savage.

## The monsters

### 1. Amomongo (regular)
- Origin: Negros Occidental, Philippines (Mount Kanlaon)
- Looks: A man-sized white or hairy ape with long nails that lives in caves.
- Behavior: Attacks livestock and eats their insides; locals treat it as an animal, not a spirit.
- Type: brute
- Reads at game size: yes: hairy ape
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amomongo (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Sensitivity note: unverified, one source (Wikipedia only); second source to be checked before art. 2008 reports were treated as local news; no sacred status found.
- Fitter's note: Clawed forest brute. Philippines.

### 2. Nogitsune (regular)
- Origin: Japanese folklore, Japan
- Looks: Wild fox with several tails and a fox-fire glow.
- Behavior: Tricks travelers, shapeshifts into people, and possesses victims, causing illness.
- Type: ranged
- Reads at game size: yes: fox with many tails
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kitsune (read 2026-10-29) | https://sites.pitt.edu/~dash/japan.html (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: yellow
- Sensitivity note: Kitsune serve the deity Inari, a living Shinto tradition; use only the wild nogitsune form, never an Inari fox or shrine imagery.
- Fitter's note: Ranged fox trickster, fox-fire. Japan, yellow.

### 3. Swarm of Spiders (regular)
- D&D SRD monster: Medium Beast, SRD 5.1. License CC BY 4.0: the game's credits need the SRD credit. Its source is the SRD itself (https://api.open5e.com/v1/monsters/swarm-of-spiders/); no folklore second source is needed.
- Fitter's note: SRD 5.1, credit required. Chitin swarm; the Spider Queen's brood.

### 4. Kalanoro (regular)
- Origin: Malagasy, Madagascar
- Looks: A small, hairy, fanged being with backward-facing feet and red-glowing eyes.
- Behavior: Solitary; some steal, kill or abduct, others serve as healers.
- Type: swarm
- Reads at game size: yes: small red-eyed hairy imp
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalanoro (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: yellow
- Sensitivity note: Unverified, one source. Said to be linked to ancestors (Vazimba); use only the small forest imp.
- Fitter's note: Small swarm of hairy forest sprites. Madagascar, yellow.

### 5. Leshy (elite)
- Origin: Slavic lands, Russia
- Looks: A forest spirit that can change size or look like a tree, stump or animal; unruly hair and beard, odd eyes, may limp.
- Behavior: Leads travelers astray, mimics voices, steals children, gambles away forest animals and punishes the disrespectful.
- Type: brute
- Reads at game size: yes: tree-like man with green hair
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leshy (read 2026-10-29) | https://neonvagabond.xyz/mirrors/sacred-texts/neu/srp/srp06.htm (read 2026-10-29) | https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/22373/pg22373.txt (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Fitter's note: Shape-shifting forest warden. Slavic.

### 6. Sasabonsam (elite)
- Origin: Akan (Ashanti), Ghana
- Looks: A tree-dwelling being with iron teeth, long hair, bloodshot eyes and feet pointing both ways.
- Behavior: Perches in high boughs, dangles its legs to snare hunters and strikes from above.
- Type: brute
- Reads at game size: yes: dangling legs from above, iron fangs
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sasabonsam (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: yellow
- Sensitivity note: Unverified, one source. Akan folk belief with ongoing local currency; keep it a generic forest ambusher.
- Fitter's note: Hook-footed tree dweller. Akan, yellow.

### 7. Humbaba (boss)
- Origin: Mesopotamia (Sumer, Babylon)
- Looks: Mountain-man giant guardian of the cedar forest with seven auras and a terrifying voice.
- Behavior: Guards the forest until Gilgamesh and Enkidu kill him.
- Type: brute
- Reads at game size: yes: giant with a roaring face
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humbaba (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Sensitivity note: Unverified, one source. Epic figure.
- Fitter's note: Level 50. Guardian of the Cedar Forest in Gilgamesh; ancient-forest anchor. Baba Yaga would repeat the Slavic elite.

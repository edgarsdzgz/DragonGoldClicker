Elderbough Crown, round 1: seven monsters

Feral fey of a misty forest, levels 41 to 50. Idle, hit, hurt, walk and death at all three Unmaking tiers. Every folklore monster found a second source (the Sasabonsam's is a weak blog, see README); D&D SRD: the Swarm of Spiders (needs the SRD credit). · 2026-10-29

v1 beside v2

Left of each pair, v1 (kept in out_v1/); right, v2. The v1 Nogitsune's tails stood up like raised arms; the v1 Swarm read as brown crumbs.

Amomongo (regular, brute)

Negros hairy ape: a man-sized white ape with long nails. Second source: a SunStar news feature (hairy white ape, long sharp nails, attacks on livestock and two men). Treated as an animal, not a spirit.

Nogitsune (regular, ranged)

The wild fox of Japanese folklore, never an Inari fox or shrine fox: tall ears, a white muzzle, four tails tipped with teal fox-fire. Second source: Pitt (Dash) yokai page, listed in the brief.

Swarm of Spiders (regular) [D&D SRD 5.1, CC BY 4.0: needs the SRD credit]

Many small brown spiders heaped together with one bigger in the middle; the Spider Queen's brood. No crown, no purple: it must not read as another Spider Queen (see the line-up).

Kalanoro (regular, swarm)

Small hairy forest imp with fangs, glowing eyes (gold here, as every monster's) and feet turned backward, only the small imp, never the Vazimba ancestors. Second source: PBS NOVA (hairy, backward feet, eyes like coals).

Leshy (elite, brute)

Slavic forest warden: bark body, green leaf hair and beard, twig antlers, one big eye and one small. Sources in the brief: three (Wikipedia, sacred-texts, Gutenberg).

Sasabonsam (elite, brute)

Akan tree dweller, kept a generic forest ambusher: perched on a bough, iron-grey fangs, long pale hair, long legs dangling with the feet hooked both ways. Second source is weak: a vampires.com blog post (tree dweller, iron teeth in some accounts, winged), so treat it as lightly verified.

Humbaba (boss, level 50)

Guardian of the Cedar Forest in Gilgamesh: a giant with a lion's grimace and a dragon's maw, seven rays of terror above his head, a bronze breastplate and an uprooted cedar. Second source: World History Encyclopedia (dragon's maw, lion's face, seven terrors). Never a god: only the creature.

# Stats as a tome (2026-10-26)

The owner asked for Stats as a magic book where records are kept, different from what we have,
and to see every book and scroll icon with what it is for. The page does both.

Taken already: the Librarium book (closed, spine view, purple with a gold diamond: the Librarium
building, banner and research) and the Journal scroll (the Journal button: Lirae's notes). The
map is parchment but folded. In code today both the Librarium and the Journal use Ionicon `book`.

Four tomes, each with a different color and silhouette:

- A floating grimoire: open, a glowing trend on the right page, sparkles above.
- B tome with ribbon bookmarks: brown leather face-on, gold sigil, three ribbons of rising length.
- C the Chronicle on its lectern: open book on a wooden stand, glowing ink.
- D stack of ledgers: green on sea on brown.

Grid: `src/tomes.px`. Build and page: `python build_page.py`, published over the options link.

The bestiary

Eighteen new monsters from the old Draconia bestiary, in the simple monster style with the busts' shading: light on the upper left, dark on the right, no blending. Each has idle, hit, hurt, walk and death at all three Unmaking tiers, built by the locked monsters' own code. Bosses vary in size. The existing six are shaded too, as v2 · 2026-10-27

City light levels

The city brightens as I rebuild it: night, dusk, lit. Built from the locked city map, plot pieces and buildings with the toolkit's night and dusk light maps (Batch 2), never redrawn · 2026-10-27

A city in ruins

Every plot ruined or bare, at each level. At night only fire and the brightest gold keep their light, so a ruined city goes dark.

A city restored

Every plot built. The lit windows, the forge and the torches stay bright at night: the rebuilt city glows.

How the game could choose

My suggestion, for the code: the light follows how much of the city is restored. Night while fewer than a third of the plots are built, dusk below two thirds, lit after that. Each piece the city screen draws has a _night and a _dusk file (46 pieces, 92 files), so the switch is a choice of file, with no color filter at run time.

Varn, Ashara, Thornwick, Sera and the Hollow Knight, in the locked simpler style: 22x23, shown at x2, light from the upper left. Drawn from their Unique Recruit entries in the design doc. v2: Thornwick is a gnome and Sera is bearfolk, peoples no bust or townsfolk sprite uses yet (v1, both human, is on board_v1.html) · 2026-10-27

Varn, Dragon Recruit

A young dragon, barely fledged: a big head, wing nubs, wide eyes, and a wooden practice sword. "I want to fight like you."

Ashara, Elder Wyrm

Ancient, tired, angry: ash-gray scales, one horn broken off, an old wound, the brow drawn down over burning eyes, fire behind her teeth, smoke rising.

Thornwick, the Exiled Mage (v2: a gnome)

Wry and reckless, now a gnome: small under a huge hat, ears out to the sides, a round nose, a white beard with singed ends, goggles on the hat band, a spark in his hand.

Sera the Shieldmaiden (v2: bearfolk)

The old guard, now bearfolk: round ears beside a small helm, a broad snout gone gray with age, fur braids, mail, and her round shield.

The Hollow Knight

Nothing shows through the visor, by design. A great helm with a dent, a black plume, a torn cloak.

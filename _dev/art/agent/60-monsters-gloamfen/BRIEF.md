# Brief: Gloamfen Mire (levels 51-60, element void)

Land: The dead and the hollow of a dark fen.

## The monsters

### 1. Jiangshi (regular)
- Origin: Chinese folklore, China
- Looks: Stiff greenish-gray corpse in an official's robe with a paper talisman on the forehead.
- Behavior: Hunts the living for their vital energy, can hop or fly when old, and fears peach wood, mirrors and a rooster's crow.
- Type: brute
- Reads at game size: yes: arms out, talisman, tall hat
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiangshi (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Sensitivity note: Widely used in fiction. Unverified, one source.
- Fitter's note: Hopping corpse. China. Near the existing Skeleton in role; the hop and the talisman make it distinct.

### 2. Adze (regular)
- Origin: Ewe, Togo and Ghana
- Looks: A firefly in the wild that becomes human when caught.
- Behavior: Slips through cracks at night to drink blood and possess people.
- Type: swarm
- Reads at game size: yes as a glowing firefly swarm
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adze_(folklore) (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: yellow
- Sensitivity note: Unverified, one source. Living belief; accusations of being an adze fall on women, the elderly and the poor. Use the firefly swarm only; never the possessed human.
- Fitter's note: Swarm: firefly that turns to a bloodsucker. Ewe, yellow.

### 3. Will-o'-Wisp (regular)
- D&D SRD monster: Tiny | Small Undead, SRD 5.1 | SRD 5.2. License CC BY 4.0: the game's credits need the SRD credit. Its source is the SRD itself (https://api.open5e.com/v1/monsters/will-o-wisp/ | https://api.open5e.com/v2/creatures/srd-2024_will-o-wisp/); no folklore second source is needed.
- Fitter's note: SRD 5.2, credit required. Spectral fen light. A folk concept that fits the fen; no folklore row.

### 4. Kikimora (regular)
- Origin: Slavic lands
- Looks: A small hunched hag with a sharp nose and tangled hair in moss, or any animal-featured face.
- Behavior: A house spirit that wrecks dishes and whistles in a neglected home. Swamp ones knock travelers off roads and drown them.
- Type: brute
- Reads at game size: yes: hunched hag with spindle
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kikimora (read 2026-10-29) | https://neonvagabond.xyz/mirrors/sacred-texts/neu/srp/srp06.htm (read 2026-10-29) | https://neonvagabond.xyz/mirrors/sacred-texts/neu/srp/srp07.htm (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Sensitivity note: Benevolent when a house is kept; the swamp form is dangerous.
- Fitter's note: Slavic marsh brute.

### 5. Draugr (elite)
- Origin: Iceland and Norway
- Looks: A swollen corpse, death-blue or corpse-pale, strong and foul-smelling. The Norwegian sea draugr is a drowned fisherman with a seaweed head in oilskins.
- Behavior: Guards its mound and treasure, grows heavy and crushes sleepers, kills stock and shepherds, and curses. Heroes wrestle it back into its grave; beheading and burning stop it.
- Type: brute
- Reads at game size: yes: bloated blue-grey figure
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Draugr (read 2026-10-29) | https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/347/pg347.txt (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Sensitivity note: Second source is Grettir's Saga (Glam episode, Gutenberg 347).
- Fitter's note: Norse grave-brute. Near Skeleton, but armored and huge.

### 6. Empousa (elite)
- Origin: Ancient Greek myth, Greece
- Looks: A shapeshifter with hair of flame and one leg of bronze and one of a donkey.
- Behavior: Sent by Hekate to frighten travelers; may take a lovely woman's form to lure and feed on young men; flees when insulted.
- Type: spectral
- Reads at game size: yes: flame hair and mismatched legs read at a glance.
- Sources so far: https://www.theoi.com/Phasma/Empousai.html (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Sensitivity note: Classical myth, widely used in fiction. Unverified, one source.
- Fitter's note: Greek one-legged spectral shapeshifter.

### 7. Dullahan (mini-boss)
- Origin: Ireland
- Looks: A headless rider on a black horse who carries his own grinning head; sometimes a coachman on a death coach.
- Behavior: Appears at the door of the soon-to-die. Some tales say blood is thrown on anyone who opens the coach door.
- Type: spectral
- Reads at game size: yes: rider with head under arm
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dullahan (read 2026-10-29) | https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/39752/pg39752.txt (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Sensitivity note: Popular form is largely from Croker's 1828 collection; the Irish Folklore Commission archive holds few reports. Only one source read (Wikipedia citing Croker);
- Fitter's note: Level 55. Headless rider. Ireland.

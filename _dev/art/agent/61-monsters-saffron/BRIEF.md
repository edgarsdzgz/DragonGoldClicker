# Brief: Saffron Dunes (levels 61-70, element plasma)

Land: Desert creatures under a cracked sky.

## The monsters

### 1. Serpopard (regular)
- Origin: Predynastic Egypt and Uruk Mesopotamia
- Looks: A lioness-bodied creature with a very long serpent neck.
- Behavior: Shown restrained on Narmer-era palettes, as a symbol of chaos beyond the borders.
- Type: brute
- Reads at game size: yes: long-necked cat
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serpopard (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Sensitivity note: Unverified, one source. Ancient art motif; no living worship.
- Fitter's note: Serpent-necked leopard. Egypt.

### 2. Nasnas (regular)
- Origin: Arabia
- Looks: A half-human with half a head, one arm and one leg.
- Behavior: Hops quickly through the desert.
- Type: swarm
- Reads at game size: yes: one-legged hopper
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasnas (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: yellow
- Sensitivity note: Unverified, one source. Occasionally used to disparage a religious group, so avoid sectarian dress.
- Fitter's note: Half-bodied hopper. Arabia, yellow.

### 3. Mummy (regular)
- D&D SRD monster: Medium | Small Undead, SRD 5.1 | SRD 5.2. License CC BY 4.0: the game's credits need the SRD credit. Its source is the SRD itself (https://api.open5e.com/v1/monsters/mummy/ | https://api.open5e.com/v2/creatures/srd-2024_mummy/); no folklore second source is needed.
- Fitter's note: SRD 5.2, credit required. Wrapped desert dead. Fills a shielded/armored gap.

### 4. Bungisngis (regular)
- Origin: Philippine folklore, Luzon (Bataan, Batangas)
- Looks: A one-eyed giant with two tusks that is always laughing.
- Behavior: Strong enough to hurl a buffalo but easily tricked and quick to panic.
- Type: brute
- Reads at game size: yes: single eye plus tusks
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bungisngis (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Sensitivity note: unverified, one source (Wikipedia only); second source to be checked before art. No sacred or ritual role noted.
- OWNER RULING 2026-10-29: replaces the Yara-ma-yha-who in this slot.

### 5. Girtablilu (elite)
- Origin: Mesopotamia (Babylon) and Iran
- Looks: Man's head, torso and arms on a scorpion's body and tail.
- Behavior: Created by Tiamat in Enuma Elish; guard the gate of Mashu with a deadly glance.
- Type: brute
- Reads at game size: yes: strong silhouette
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girtablilu (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Sensitivity note: Unverified, one source. Ancient religion, now extinct.
- Fitter's note: Scorpion-person of Babylon. Chitin.

### 6. Poludnitsa (elite)
- Origin: Slavic lands
- Looks: A tall woman in white who appears at noon; the field spirit has mismatched eyes and grass for hair.
- Behavior: Pulls the hair of field workers at midday, leads children astray in the corn, tramples sleepers and causes illness.
- Type: spectral
- Reads at game size: yes: white figure in heat haze
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polevik (read 2026-10-29) | https://neonvagabond.xyz/mirrors/sacred-texts/neu/srp/srp06.htm (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Fitter's note: Noonday heat spirit. Slavic.

### 7. Manticore (mini-boss)
- Origin: Persia
- Looks: Human face, lion body, scorpion tail, three rows of teeth.
- Behavior: Eats victims whole, leaving no bones.
- Type: ranged
- Reads at game size: yes: lion with a stinging tail
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manticore (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Sensitivity note: Unverified, one source. Widely used in fiction. Whether the D&D SRD lists it was not checked (unverified); the folklore license is green regardless.
- Fitter's note: Level 65. Spine-flinging ranged beast. Persia. Ammit (rated green) is left out: she is an Egyptian deity-demon, so the owner should judge.

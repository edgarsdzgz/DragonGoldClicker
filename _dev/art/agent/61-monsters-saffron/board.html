Saffron Dunes: seven monsters, v2 (v1 beside v2 for four)

Idle, hit, hurt, walk and death at all three Unmaking tiers. Every folklore monster found a second source (see each note); where only the Wikipedia page backs a detail, the note says so. SRD monster: the Mummy (credit needed). · 2026-10-29

Serpopard (regular)

v1 (left, kept in out_v1/) beside v2. Second source: the Two Dog Palette page (talesfromthetwolands.org) confirms the very long serpentine neck; the Narmer-palette detail stays on the Wikipedia source only. A lean lioness with a long banded neck, teal so it stands off the sand.

Nasnas (regular)

v1 (left, kept in out_v1/) beside v2. Second source: mythicalencyclopedia.com quoting Edward Lane (half a head, half a body, one arm, one leg, hops). Half a figure with a dark hollow where the other half should be, no sectarian dress.

Mummy (regular) [D&D SRD 5.2, CC BY 4.0: the credits need the SRD credit]

Pale gray wrappings cut by ink gaps, an azure collar, arms raised, trailing strips. Wrapped head and no bones, so it does not read as the Skeleton.

Bungisngis (regular)

v1 (left, kept in out_v1/) beside v2. Second source: pantheon.org (one eye in the forehead, constant laughter, hurls a carabao, easily tricked). Tusks are on the Wikipedia source only. Blue giant, one big gold eye, two tusks, a laughing mouth.

Girtablilu (elite)

Second source: godsandmonsters.info (made by Tiamat, guards the gate of Mashu, deadly gaze). Bearded man on a green chitin scorpion body, pincers raised, tail arched over. Only the creature, never a god.

Poludnitsa (elite)

Two sources already (Polevik; the sacred-texts Serbian chapter). A tall figure in a white gown, grass hair, mismatched eyes (gold and pale blue), the hem rippling like heat.

Manticore (mini-boss)

v1 (left, kept in out_v1/) beside v2. Second source: theoi.com (human face, lion body, three rows of teeth in each jaw, scorpion tail, man-eater). A violet lion with a human face in an orange mane and an arched stinger tail. D&D SRD status not checked.

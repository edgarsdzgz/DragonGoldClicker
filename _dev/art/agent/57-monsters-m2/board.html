Monster batch M2: the Impundulu, Omukade and Tempest Lord

Mistral Peaks' two elites and its boss, built like M1: idle, hit, hurt, walk and death at all three Unmaking tiers. · 2026-10-29

Impundulu

Zulu lightning bird, an elite flier: a person-sized black-and-white storm bird with a hammer-shaped crest, a white breast, black wings spread and pale yellow-white lightning off the wingtips and talons. No red. The walk is a wingbeat, and it has a shadow.

Omukade

Japanese giant mountain centipede, an elite of the chitin type: a broad armored head with curved mandibles and gold eyes, a segmented body reared up in a gentle S, many legs that ripple in the walk, a coiled base. Dark bronze shell, not red.

Tempest Lord of the Peaks

The boss (level 30), first of the Storm-Callers: a tall hooded priest, human-born but no longer a man. Arms raised, a crown of lightning, a staff, below the waist a churning storm funnel in place of legs. In the walk the cloud churns and the robes whip. No shadow.

The Tempest Lord beside the Windcaller and the Storm Rider

He must read as a different, bigger monster: a storm funnel where the legs should be.

Size line-up

Kamaitachi (M1), an M1 elite-size reference (the Gargoyle), the two new elites, then the Basilisk and the Tempest Lord.

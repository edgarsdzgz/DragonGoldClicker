Four bosses, sized like the locked ones (beasts bigger than the human-shaped), built exactly like the locked bestiary: shaded from the upper left, no blending, idle, hit, hurt, walk and death at all three Unmaking tiers. The last batch of the second round. · 2026-10-27

Beasts

Basilisk

A crowned serpent-lizard: a frill of gold spikes, eyes burning white-gold, a low splayed body, a curling tail (24x20). Hurt, a crown spike breaks.

Spider Queen

The Shadow Crawlers' mother: a bloated abdomen carrying pale egg sacs, eight eyes, fangs, eight long legs (26x20). She dies on her back, like the crawler.

Corrupted Drake

The Ember Wraith's great cousin: thick swept horns, cracked charcoal scales with fire in the seams and jaws, small raised wings, far smaller than the Wyvern's (24x22).

The risen dead

Lich

The master of the Skeletons and Spectral Hounds: a crowned skull in a high-collared robe, a staff with a pale green orb, magic in its bony hand (20x25).

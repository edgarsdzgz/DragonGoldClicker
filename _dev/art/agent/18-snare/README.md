# Snares and Nets as a box trap (2026-10-26)

The round rope net read as a waffle or a basket. The owner sent references of the classic box
trap (a wooden box or crate propped on one edge by a forked stick, a string tied at its foot,
bait underneath) and asked for that. Bait is a carrot, not cheese, since the Food icon is the
cheese wedge. A with the carrot, B without bait, C tipped up more steeply.

Grids: `src/make_options.py` writes `src/options.px`. Build and page: `python build_page.py`.

Round 2: the owner sent rope-snare references (a spring snare on a bent sapling, a noose on a
crossbar between forked posts, a stake and loop held open by a forked stick). D spring snare,
E noose on a branch, F snare on a stake. Posts and a ground strip were tried first and dropped:
at 12 pixels they closed the noose up into a dark ring.

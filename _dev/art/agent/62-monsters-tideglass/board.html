Blue Man of the Minch (regular)

Scotland, the Minch. A swarm of blue half-figures with gray faces and long arms, chest-deep in the waves, arms thrown up. Signature color: bright blue skin over dark-blue water. Two sources (Wikipedia, Gutenberg).

Funayurei (regular)

Japan. The drowned sailor: a cream burial robe, a white forehead cloth, black hair plastered down, hollow gold eyes, a huge wooden ladle held out for the ship's bucket, and sea-teal wisps in place of legs. Not the Wraith. [UNVERIFIED] No second source read (yokai.jp refused with 403, a second site answered 429).

Archelon (regular)

[SRD 5.2, CC BY 4.0: the game's credits need the SRD credit] A giant sea turtle: a green plated dome shell, a sandy rim and head, a hooked beak. The shield of the Land.

Stymphalian Birds (regular, flier)

Greece. A bronze bird of prey: steel beak and talons, wings whose feathers end in steel arrowheads. Two sources (theoi.com, Pausanias on Perseids). Flier: a wingbeat walk and a shadow.

Fomorian Raider (elite)

Irish myth. A generic sea-raider giant (never Balor, Elatha or Bres): a goat's head with curling horns, one huge arm hauling an anchor, one withered arm, one thick leg and one peg leg, kelp rags. Two sources (Wikipedia, encyclopedia.com).

Sirens (elite, flier)

Greece. A bird-woman: v2: a woman's head and shoulders with long cream hair and a mouth open in song, a feathered green body with feathered thighs, big hooked talons, wings rising from her shoulders. Two sources (theoi.com, Audubon). Flier: a wingbeat walk and a shadow.

Nuckelavee (mini-boss, level 75)

Orkney. v2, front-on: a skinless horse coming at us, one great gold eye and a huge open mouth, a rider's torso fused to its back with an oversized lolling head and arms past the knees, raw clay muscle with pale sinew. Two sources (Wikipedia, folklorescotland.com).

Kraken (boss, level 80)

Norway and Iceland. Owner's staging: it rises up a flooded stretch of road. A vast mantle with one great gold eye lifts out of black water over the cobbles, tentacles reaching left toward the gate. Two sources (Wikipedia, Store norske leksikon).

Size line-up

Kobold, Kamaitachi, the regulars, the elites, the Basilisk, the Nuckelavee and the Kraken, the widest of all.

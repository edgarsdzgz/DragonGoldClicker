# Brief: Tideglass Coast (levels 71-80, element lightning)

Land: Sea raiders on a stormy coast.

## The monsters

### 1. Blue Men of the Minch (regular)
- Origin: Scotland, Outer Hebrides (the Minch)
- Looks: Human-sized sea beings with blue skin, grey faces and long arms, floating chest-deep in the water.
- Behavior: Raise storms and demand a verse answer from a skipper; if it fails, they try to capsize the ship. One tale holds them to be fallen angels.
- Type: swarm
- Reads at game size: yes: blue half-figures rising from waves
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_men_of_the_Minch (read 2026-10-29) | https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/61730/pg61730.txt (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Fitter's note: Swarm of storm-sea men. Scotland.

### 2. Funayurei (regular)
- Origin: Japanese folklore, Japan
- Looks: Glowing ghosts of the drowned, or ghost lights and phantom ships, over a stormy sea.
- Behavior: Appear on foggy or stormy nights, ask ships for a ladle to flood them, and lure boats to danger with false lights.
- Type: spectral
- Reads at game size: yes: pale figure with ladle; overlaps Wraith in role
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funayūrei (read 2026-10-29) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_legendary_creatures_from_Japan (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Sensitivity note: Ghost folklore, widely used.
- Fitter's note: Spectral drowned sailor. Japan.

### 3. Archelon (regular)
- D&D SRD monster: Huge Beast, SRD 5.2. License CC BY 4.0: the game's credits need the SRD credit. Its source is the SRD itself (https://api.open5e.com/v2/creatures/srd-2024_archelon/); no folklore second source is needed.
- Fitter's note: SRD 5.2, credit required. Giant sea turtle: shielded.

### 4. Stymphalian Birds (regular)
- Origin: Ancient Greek myth, Greece
- Looks: Man-eating birds of prey with bronze wings that can be flung like arrows.
- Behavior: Gather in huge flocks in a dense wood by a lake and strip the land; scared into the air by loud rattles, then shot.
- Type: flier
- Reads at game size: yes: flock of small winged shapes, good swarm.
- Sources so far: https://www.theoi.com/Ther/OrnithesStymphalides.html (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Sensitivity note: Classical myth, widely used in fiction. Unverified, one source.
- Fitter's note: Flier: bronze-feathered flock, rated event in the catalog; rank is a call for the owner.

### 5. Fomorian Raider (elite)
- Origin: Ireland (Irish myth cycles)
- Looks: Sea-borne giants with grotesque bodies; one account gives a goat's head, another one eye, one arm and one leg.
- Behavior: Rise from under the sea to demand tribute of grain, cattle and children. Later the word meant any sea raider.
- Type: brute
- Reads at game size: yes: tall misshapen figure with mismatched limbs
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fomorians (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: yellow
- Sensitivity note: Leaders (Balor, Elatha, Bres) are mythic figures tied to gods; use the generic raiders only. unverified, one source
- Fitter's note: Sea-raider brute. Irish myth, yellow.

### 6. Sirens (elite)
- Origin: Ancient Greek myth, Greece
- Looks: Part woman, part bird, sitting among the corpses of sailors.
- Behavior: Lure ships onto the shore with enchanting song.
- Type: flier
- Reads at game size: yes: bird-woman silhouette.
- Sources so far: https://www.theoi.com/Pontios/Seirenes.html (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Sensitivity note: Classical myth, widely used in fiction. Unverified, one source.
- Fitter's note: Flier; song luring. Greek.

### 7. Nuckelavee (mini-boss)
- Origin: Scotland, Orkney
- Looks: A skinless horse-like sea demon with a man's torso grown from its back and one red eye; sinew shows pale and raw.
- Behavior: Comes ashore to blight crops and sicken stock. It will not come in rain and cannot cross fresh running water.
- Type: brute
- Reads at game size: yes: centaur-like red-and-pale figure
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuckelavee (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Sensitivity note: unverified, one source
- Fitter's note: Level 75. Skinless sea-horse rider. Orkney.

### 8. Kraken (boss)
- Origin: Norway and Iceland
- Looks: A vast sea monster with many arms or heads; its back looks like skerries with weed.
- Behavior: Drags boats down, lures fish, and sinks to make a whirlpool. Saying its name sends it back down.
- Type: brute
- Reads at game size: yes: tentacles rising from a shoreline
- Sources so far: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraken (read 2026-10-29)
- Sensitivity: green
- Sensitivity note: Giant squid sightings are a likely root.  unverified, one source
- Fitter's note: Level 80. Norse. The SRD also has a Kraken, but the folklore version needs no credit. Akkorokamui, Te Wheke-a-Muturangi, Bakunawa and Makara are red.
- OWNER RULING 2026-10-29: the Kraken rises up a flooded stretch of road: its head and one great eye lift out of dark water on the road, tentacles reaching ahead toward the gate. It still advances like any boss.

Kamaitachi

Japanese folklore, the sickle weasel. A lean weasel reared up with both forelimbs ending in steel sickles, bristled spines, a long tail, riding a whirlwind. A regular (swarm) monster of Mistral Peaks. The walk swaps the swirl and the tail; hurt, the left blade breaks.

Monster batch M1: the Aitvaras, Gargoyle and Wild Hunt Rider

Three more regulars of Mistral Peaks, built like the Kamaitachi: idle, hit, hurt, walk and death at all three Unmaking tiers. · 2026-10-29

Aitvaras

Lithuanian folklore: a black rooster that flies as a dragon in the open. Comb, gold eyes and beak, spread wings, a serpent body trailing a long S-curved flame like a meteor. Fire is orange and gold only. A flier: the walk is a wingbeat, and it has a shadow.

Gargoyle

D&D SRD monster (CC BY 4.0), the shielded flier: a crouched, horned stone body in ash gray, its heavy slab wings held forward over its front like a shield, ribbed like armor. A flier: the walk lifts the wings, and it has a shadow.

Wild Hunt Rider

Germanic and Norse folklore: an anonymous spectral huntsman, wide bone antlers on a dark hood, a hunting horn, on a heavy black ghost horse with glowing eyes and a black hound at its side. A ground rider: it walks, no shadow.

Rider against the locked Storm Rider

The Wild Hunt Rider is kept only if it reads as a different monster: antlers, a hound, a heavy black horse on the ground, against the Storm Rider's gray wind horse and lance.

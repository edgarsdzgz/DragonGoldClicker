# Gems and the hammer (2026-10-26)

Dragon Anatomy: the owner liked the crystal shard and asked for a prettier, well-cut, glittering
magic stone that stands out among the gold and brown, in green, electric blue, pink, magenta and
teal. The palette had no such colors, so five gem ramps were added to the starter palette
(`00-foundation/pixkit/palette.py`: emerald, azure, rose, magenta, teal; dark to highlight).
Three cuts, each in the five colors: the shard, the brilliant (side view) and the emerald cut (top).

Tempered Steel: the owner liked hammer and anvil with the hammer in the foreground, and a better
hammer. Reference (MIT OCW, Modern Blacksmithing): a cross-peen hammer has a flat round face that
spreads metal every way and a blunt wedge peen across the handle that draws it one way. Two
layouts: the hammer lying in front, and leaning across the front.

Mortar Craft took round 2's trowel (after the owner's reference).

Grids: `src/make_options.py`. Build and page: `python build_page.py`, published over the options link.

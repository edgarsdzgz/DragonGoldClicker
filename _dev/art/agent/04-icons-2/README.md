# Icon sheet 2: padlock, flag, compass, upgrade arrow, check mark (2026-10-26)

The next five icons after the official set: shown all the time in the game, with no pixel version
yet. Grids: `src/icons.px`. Build: `python build.py`. Sheet: `preview/sheet_x6.png`.

| Icon | Used for | What the research said | The icon |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Padlock | Locked buildings, tabs, features | A padlock is a body, a shackle that wraps the thing secured, and a keyway in the body. | Steel shackle arch on two legs; brass body; keyhole. |
| Flag | The objective strip | Medieval pennons are pointed or swallow-tailed; banners hang from a vertical pole and may have a cut tail. | Swallow-tailed banner on a wooden pole, gold finial. Brick red, because pure red is kept for HP and danger. |
| Compass | Explorers, expeditions | No pixel compass found in the packs searched. | Brass pocket compass with its bow on top, cream face, needle red to the north-east and steel to the south-west, ink pivot. |
| Upgrade arrow | Buildings that can level up | Small UI icons hold up with a bold silhouette, a dark outline and flat shading. | A bold arrow in ember gold, the key-action color. |
| Check mark | Done, finished objectives | Same: thick strokes read; thin ones do not. | A thick two-stroke check in green, lit on its top edges. |

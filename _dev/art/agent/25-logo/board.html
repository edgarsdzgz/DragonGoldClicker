The Draconia Chronicles logo

One emblem as both the logo and the app icon. The protagonist stays with the artist, so the dragon appears only by its marks: its claws, its ember, the city it guards. Icons are 64x64 pixels, scaled by 16 to the stores' 1024x1024, with the emblem inside the middle the phones never crop. The wordmark is the same for all three · 2026-10-27

Chosen: A is the logo and app icon, B is the splash

The splash fades in from the dark one piece at a time in palette steps (silhouette, dusk, lit), 5.5 seconds in all, ending on a hold. A also has an intro of its own: the shield slams in from the foreground, the claws rip upward, the debris settles, about 3 seconds.

A: The claw-struck shield

The city's shield, struck by three ember claw marks. The dragon is there only by its mark: it defends the city, and its strike is the game's tap.

B: The gate under the ember moon

Draconia's gate and towers, the hearth glowing behind the portcullis, a gold moon rising. The city the dragon guards, the place the whole game defends.

C: The D monogram

A bold gold D with Pyreth's ember burning in its heart. The simplest to read at small sizes, and it pairs directly with the wordmark.

RAXDEG STUDIOS

The studio's startup splash, before the game's own. The shield belongs to the game, so the studio takes Raxdeg's own wax seal, with the shield's slam and slash. Raxdeg is Edgar, with an X; code is the spell, and calling it casts it.

The seal intro, in three finishes

The seal flies in from the foreground, unlit, and slams down in a ring of dust. Then the spell ignites: a white-hot wave runs out from the center knot to the outer ring and the seal cools to its finish. Last, the slash rips left to right and carves RAXDEG·STVDIOS. About 4 seconds, then it holds.

The runic face, from the seal

One-pixel strokes and angular letters like the seal's runes, a dot inside the A and the O like the seal's dots, a V for U the way old inscriptions carve it, and a dot between the words.

Earlier: the shield version

The shield's slam and claws, then one more slash carves the name. The shield is now the game's alone; this stays for reference.

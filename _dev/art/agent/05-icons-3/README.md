# Icon sheet 3: ten interface icons (2026-10-26)

The next ten icons the game shows with no pixel version: from the Warfront (shield, gate, damage,
skull), Forage (rations, bell, train, reassign), and the general interface (close, book). Grids:
`src/icons.px`. Build: `python build.py`. Sheet: `preview/sheet_x6.png`.

| Icon | Replaces Ionicon | What the research said | The icon |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shield (Fighters) | shield | The heater shield: a flat top, sides curving to a point; the classic shape for showing arms. | Steel rim, army slate-blue field, gold diamond. Fighters no longer borrow the swords. |
| Gate | log-in | (No gatehouse source came back; drawn from the castle icon's language.) | Three merlons, a stone wall, an arched opening with wooden doors and iron bands. |
| Damage | flash | Small icons need one strong silhouette. | A zigzag bolt in glow colors. |
| Skull | skull | At 16 px a skull is a dome, two dark sockets and a hint of teeth; three bone tones; pure white flattens it. | Exactly that, plus a nose hole. |
| Rations | restaurant | Medieval pottage was a vegetable stew eaten from turned wooden bowls. | A wooden bowl of pottage with herbs and a wooden spoon. |
| Bell | notifications-outline | (Hand-bell sources were replica listings only.) | A brass bell with a knob, a flared lip and the clapper. |
| Train | person-add | Convention: a person plus a plus sign. | The villager icon's person with a gold plus. |
| Reassign | swap-horizontal | Convention: two opposing arrows. | Two steel arrows, right over left. |
| Close | close | Convention: an X. | A bold steel X. |
| Book (Librarium) | library, book | A closed book reads with its spine on one side and the page edges showing. | Dark spine with gold bands, Lirae's ink-blue cover with a gold diamond, page edges on the right. |

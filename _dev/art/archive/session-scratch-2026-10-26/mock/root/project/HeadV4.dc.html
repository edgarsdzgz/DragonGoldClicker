The hide is darker than before, so the eye and the throat fire are the brightest points.

The reference rules, applied

  • Three-quarter view: the lit top of the muzzle and the far brow ridge show above the bridge.
  • Eye in the middle: eye-to-snout 1.0× eye-to-skull (V2 was 1.7×).
  • Brow step: the muzzle drops away from a heavy brow, and the snout tapers.
  • Big back of the head: skull, jaw muscle, three cheek spikes.
  • Crest: three horns and six spikes fanning back on the snout-to-horn diagonal.
  • Neck: as thick as V2, but leaving the jaw corner down and back, so the head is held forward.
  • Mouth: open, fangs of different sizes, fire in the throat; it ends under the eye.
  • Value: darker hide; the eye glows in a dark socket.

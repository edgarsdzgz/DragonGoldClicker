What changed from V5

  • Faces right, as a clean profile with a slight turn: only the far brow and far horn show.
  • Eye in a bony frame: a slanted almond with its upper lid across the iris, a dark socket, a brow shelf sloping toward the snout, and a cheekbone ridge below.
  • Hinged jaw: it pivots under the ear behind the eye and opens as a wedge. It is thinner than the upper jaw, with a jaw-muscle bulge behind the mouth corner.
  • Inside the mouth: a tongue, gums, a darker far row of teeth, fire deep in the throat. The lip line dips at the big teeth.
  • Snout: tapers to a rounded, hooked tip; a nostril bump, a dip behind it, then the rise to the brow, with a lit ridge along the top.
  • Kept from V5: ringed horns, crown plates, lip scales, neck scales, ear opening, skin folds.

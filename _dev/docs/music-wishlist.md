# Music wishlist: tracks to license (2026-10-28)

Today one track plays everywhere: "Refusing the Ultimatum" (Arthur Vyncke, CC BY-SA 3.0), from the title
screen on, on a loop. It stays as the main theme. Below is every place a track of its own would help, in
the order I would license them. When tracks come in, the app switches music by screen with a crossfade,
lets a boss or the Breach take over, and plays the story beds under scenes.

## What every track needs

- **Instrumental, no lyrics.** Players read the story while music plays; a voice competes with the words.
- **The same family as the main theme:** orchestral fantasy. The game's look is "a fireside fantasy
  painting in 16-bit pixel art", grand over cute. Avoid chiptune, comedy, EDM and trailer music that
  climaxes every 20 seconds.
- **Loops cleanly, 2 to 4 minutes** (a loop version, or an ending that fades well). The one-off cues are
  marked.
- **Sits under sound effects:** taps, hits and horns play over it. Prefer a steady bed over a busy, high
  melody, and a gentle master over a loud, crushed one (music plays at 40% by default).
- **The license allows a commercial mobile game.** CC BY and paid royalty-free licenses are best. Avoid
  NonCommercial (NC). ShareAlike (SA) and NoDerivatives (ND) work as long as the music is used unchanged
  (converting the format is fine). Keep the title, author, source and license link for the credits.

## The list

| # | Screen | Use | Feel | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Warfront**: the siege (main screen, most of play) | Loops under the tapping for long sessions, with torches, wind, the horde's drums and constant hits | Steady, driving resolve under pressure. Mid-tempo (about 90 to 110 BPM; the horde's drum loop is 90). Low string ostinato, frame drums, horns. Minor key with a lift, never frantic. No busy high melody | 1 |
| 2 | **Draconia**: the ruined city (the city tab while it is night, before the Keep is restored) | Building and upgrading; calm, long listening | Sparse and melancholy, with a hope that has not gone out. A solo voice (cello, harp or wooden flute) over a soft pad. Slow (60 to 80 BPM). A city at night waiting for its light | 1 |
| 3 | **Warfront**: a boss at the gate (every level of the horde ends with a boss or mini-boss) | Takes over from the siege for the fight (1 to 3 minutes), after the boss horn; loops if the fight runs long | Urgent and heavy. 120 to 140 BPM, big percussion, brass stabs, a wordless choir. A strong opening is welcome | 2 |
| 4 | **Draconia**: the city rebuilt (once the city brightens at dusk and when lit) | The same use as 2, later in the game | Warm, homely, quietly proud. Fuller: strings, harp, light percussion, major key. Firelight in windows. Best of all: the same piece as 2 in a fuller arrangement (some packs sell "calm" and "full" versions) | 2 |
| 5 | **The Breach** (the wall falls: two scenes, then the report) | One-off cue, 60 to 90 seconds, fading out; no loop needed | Devastation, then grief: heavy low hits as the wall falls, then slow strings and choir. The report shows the broken gate at dawn, so it ends on a thin thread of hope | 2 |
| 6 | **Forage**: the valley (the Forage tab) | Gathering, training gatherers, sending expeditions | Pastoral, open air, gentle movement. Folk instruments (lute, fiddle, flute, hand drum). Light and outdoorsy, never silly or cute. 90 to 110 BPM | 3 |
| 7 | **Story scenes**, hope (dialogue over any screen) | A quiet bed under the warm scenes: arrivals, gratitude, the city coming back | Tender and slow: solo piano, harp or strings. Little melody, because the words are being read | 3 |
| 8 | **Story scenes**, dread | A quiet bed under the dark scenes: warnings, the horde growing, the Unmaking | Tense and low: drones, a distant pulse, sparse percussion. Unease, not horror | 3 |
| 9 | **Warfront**: the deep Unmaking (from horde level 10, when the monsters' cracks glow) | Replaces the siege track once the horde is marked deepest | The siege's pulse, darker and wrong: dissonance, a low choir, bowed metal. A darker version of track 1 would be ideal | 4 |
| 10 | **The Librarium and the Wizard's Tower** (their interiors) | While one of those sheets is open | Mystical and curious: celesta, harp, glassy bells, a soft choir. Slow | 4 |

No track is needed for Settings, Stats or the pop-up cards: they keep the music of the screen under them.
The title screen keeps the main theme.

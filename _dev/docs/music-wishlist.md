# Music wishlist: tracks to license (2026-10-28)

Today one track plays everywhere: "Refusing the Ultimatum" (Arthur Vyncke, CC BY-SA 3.0), from the title
screen on, on a loop. It stays as the main theme. Below is every place a track of its own would help, in
the order I would license them. When tracks come in, the app switches music by screen with a crossfade,
lets a boss or the Breach take over, and plays the story beds under scenes.

## What every track needs

- **Instrumental, no lyrics.** Players read the story while music plays; a voice competes with the words.
- **The same family as the main theme:** orchestral fantasy. The game's look is "a fireside fantasy
  painting in 16-bit pixel art", grand over cute. Avoid chiptune, comedy, EDM and trailer music that
  climaxes every 20 seconds.
- **Loops cleanly, 2 to 4 minutes** (a loop version, or an ending that fades well). The one-off cues are
  marked.
- **Sits under sound effects:** taps, hits and horns play over it. Prefer a steady bed over a busy, high
  melody, and a gentle master over a loud, crushed one (music plays at 40% by default).
- **The license allows a commercial mobile game.** CC BY and paid royalty-free licenses are best. Avoid
  NonCommercial (NC). ShareAlike (SA) and NoDerivatives (ND) work as long as the music is used unchanged
  (converting the format is fine). Keep the title, author, source and license link for the credits.

No track is needed for Settings, Stats or the pop-up cards: they keep the music of the screen under them.
The title screen keeps the main theme.

# Second sources

Each monster I keep gets its folklore checked against a second source before it is drawn (the rule in
`../monster-batches.md`). One row per monster, added when it is checked.

| Monster | Batch | Second source (read) | What it confirms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kamaitachi | M1 | https://yokai.com/kamaitachi/ (2026-10-29) | A weasel with spiny fur and steel-sharp sickle claws that rides the whirlwinds of mountain regions; attacks in threes (one trips, one slashes, one heals the cut). Matches Wikipedia. |
| Aitvaras | M1 | http://www.blackdrago.com/species/aitvaras.htm (2026-10-29; cites Bane, Dixon-Kennedy, Rose and others) | A black rooster indoors; outdoors a flying dragon or a serpent with a fiery tail, seen at night as a wavy line of fire. Matches Wikipedia. |
| Wild Hunt Rider | M1 | Already three in its catalog row: Wikipedia, Ashliman's bogey page, a Project Gutenberg text (2026-10-29) | Spectral huntsmen on ghost horses with black hounds, sweeping the night sky. Drawn as an anonymous rider, never its leader (Odin, Wodan or Holda). |
| Gargoyle | M1 | The D&D SRD itself (5.1 and 5.2, CC BY 4.0) | A D&D monster, not folklore: the SRD is the source, and it needs the SRD credit in the game's credits. |

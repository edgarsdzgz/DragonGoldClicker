## Gaps and decisions

- Hollowspire Below (ice) is thin: 3 minions only, two of them SRD; a fourth is open (Winter Wolf is the SRD fallback).
- Shielded and chitin minions are rare in the folklore catalogs; Gargoyle, Mummy, Archelon and Animated Armor (shielded) and Swarm of Spiders (chitin) are SRD fills.
- Mistral boss is the lore Tempest Lord; the alternates are fallbacks only.
- Corrupted Wyvern is placed in Hollowspire because Mistral's boss is fixed by lore; swap if preferred.
- Shadowmere has no boss by design; the Rift Boss is the owner's.
- Stymphalian Birds is rated event in its catalog; placing it as a Tideglass minion is a rank call.
- Ammit is rated green but is a deity-demon; left out of picks. Lamashtu (green) is picked at Shadowmere; the owner should judge both.
- Yellow picks: Ahuizotl, Nogitsune, Kalanoro, Sasabonsam, Adze, Nasnas, Yara-ma-yha-who, Fomorian Raider, Camazotz, Sigbin, Chemosit.
- Japan appears in several Lands (one or two picks each); the owner may want to cap it.

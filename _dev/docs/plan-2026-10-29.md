# Plan after the playtest (2026-10-29)

Everything the playtest found (`playtest-2026-10-29.md`), the fix for "Gather 200 food by hand", and the
features still to build, in one ordered list. Items marked **(decide)** wait on a ruling from me.

## The 200 food: Valley Finds

**The problem.** "Gather 200 food by hand" is 200 taps on one button (or two and a half minutes of holding,
since a held orb repeats every 750 ms). Nothing moves, nothing changes, nothing to decide. Pyreth's line
promises a valley of "forest and river", and the screen shows none of it.

**Recommended: Valley Finds.** Foraging stirs things up in the valley around the orb. Each find shows for a
moment, and tapping it in time pays a bonus. Missing one costs nothing.

| Find | How you take it | Pays (provisional) | How often |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rabbit | darts across the grass; tap it once | +10 food | common |
| Berry bush | springs up; tap it 3 times before it fades | +5 a tap | common |
| Leaping fish | jumps from the river for about 1 second; tap it | +15 food | uncommon |
| Wild honey | rare; tap it 5 times while the bees circle | +25 food | about 1 find in 10 |

- **When:** one find at a time, every 8 to 12 forage taps. The first comes on the 5th tap, with a guide
  bubble: "Something moves in the grass. Tap it!"
- **Effect on the quest:** the target stays 200 (Pyreth's line says 200), but a player who catches the finds
  gets there in about 110 to 130 taps. A player who misses all of them still finishes in 200.
- **After the quest:** finds keep coming whenever I forage by hand, scaled with forage power. That fills
  the wanderer wait (8 to 14 minutes with nothing new) and keeps the Forage tab worth a visit later.
- **Counted by hand:** finds count toward `foodGatheredByHand`. That keeps the 2026-10-26 ruling that the
  stores are filled by hand.
- **Sound:** the "find (chest)" effect from sound batch 2 is already imported.
- **Accessibility:** each find is at least a 48 dp target. With Reduce Motion it stands still and stays
  longer. A screen reader announces it ("A rabbit in the grass") as a button. Nothing needs a hold or a drag.
- **Art:** a small art batch (rabbit, berry bush, fish, honeycomb and bees, each a short strip), approved
  before any code uses it. Until then the build can use the existing pixel icons as stand-ins.
- **Code:** a reusable `ValleyFind` component (sprite, timer, taps needed, reward callback) and a pure
  `types/valleyFinds.ts` (roll, pick, pay), with tests for the roll, the pay and the quest count.

**Other options, smaller or bigger:**
- **Shorten only:** 200 to 100. A one-line change plus tests and Pyreth's line. It makes the stretch shorter
  but no less dull.
- **Gatherers count:** train gatherers during the quest and count their food. The player meets idle income
  sooner, but it reverses the 2026-10-26 ruling.
- **Three valley spots** (berries that run out and regrow, the river, snares you set and check later): real
  choices, but a Forage redesign. Better as a later expansion built on Valley Finds.

**(decide)** Valley Finds (recommended), shorten only, or another option.

   header picture and icons.
4. **A Rally-or-claws hint:** not now. Decide after the balance simulator, and if anything, show both buys
   in the same terms (like the Garrison's army-time box) rather than a "buy this" nudge.
5. **The story mood tagging rule:** approved as proposed.
6. **The final boss:** stays open until the story after chapter 13 is planned; start from the dragonIdler
   lore, then `final-boss-ideas.md`.

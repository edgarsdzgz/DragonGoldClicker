# Playtest report: a fresh game on the emulator (2026-10-29)

A play-through from a wiped save to the end of the story (chapter 13), written up for how the game feels:
taps, clicks, pacing and progression. Build: commit 19af3e2 (no game changes since d3647f8), release APK
with dev tools, on the Player_2 emulator (Android 15, 1080x2424).

## How it was played

- **By hand** from the title to the Garrison, about 25 minutes: every tap on the monster, every Rally,
  Repair, forage and train, every dialogue line read.
- **With Dev grants** after that, wherever the next stretch was only tapping for renown: the Garrison,
  Forge, Keep and Librarium, and the claws from Hooked to Ancient. Each new screen was still opened and used
  by hand.
- **The last chapters (10 to 13)** with Dev's Jump, then Replay, because each waits on an event (the wall
  below half, an expedition home, horde level 10, a boss falling). Only Sera's and the Eldest's scenes were
  replayed before the emulator gave out.
- **Not reached:** a level-10 boss, the Wizard's Tower, the Footman and Knight ranks, Lirae's later
  studies, the deep Unmaking.

**Read these numbers with three caveats:**

1. **Test pacing was on.** Dev's "Story pacing" defaults to Test, which shortens the gaps between story
   beats to seconds. Scenes came back to back here; in live pacing they are hours apart. Story pacing is
   judged below from the code, not from this run.
2. **The emulator is weak.** It froze twice, crashed once and kernel-panicked once (details at the end).
   Taps were sent by script at 3 to 5 a second, faster than most thumbs.
3. **I could not hear the game.** The music and sounds were not checked.

## The short version

The first ten minutes are the best part. One kobold, one tap at a time, a quest that names the next goal,
Rally lit up the moment it is affordable, and the city turning from a fogged ruin to a green valley when
the Keep is restored. Those moments land.

Three things work against it:

- **A crash and repeated freezes.** One "Maximum update depth exceeded" crash in the strike effects, and
  four "isn't responding" hangs, all inside the pixel-art drawing (see Problems 1 and 2). The emulator makes
  these worse, but the crash is a code bug on any phone.
- **The middle is long, pure tapping.** Renown per tap is the claw damage times 0.05. Reaching the Ancient
  claws by tapping alone is about 3,300 taps, and Chapter 8 waits on it.
- **Many small rough edges** in wording, numbers and colors, listed below. None blocks play, but together
  they make the screens feel less finished than the art.

**How it feels:**
- **Rally-to-Rally:** about 0.04 renown a tap before sharpening, 0.10 after Sharpened, 0.15 after Hooked.
  Each claw level is a clear jump you feel within a few kills.
- **Monster variety:** the Shadow Crawler at about 3 minutes, then the Goblin Scout, Goblin Archer,

- **The weakest stretch is "Gather 200 food by hand":** 200 taps on one button, with no monster, no

## The middle, with grants

- **Garrison (40):** a good screen. The warning box "Your army kills an Orc in about 93 seconds. An Orc
  strikes the wall every 5." is the clearest piece of game information in the app, and it updates as you
  buy (93 s to 69 s for two conscripts). Conscript prices climb fast: 10, then 22, then 35.35.
- **Forge (75):** swords and shields with the same live army-time box (93 s to 81 s).
- **The Keep (150):** the city turns from a night ruin to a green valley. The best reward in the game so far.
- **Lodge, Librarium, Outpost:** each opens to a clear sheet with a header picture. The Librarium's five
  branches and locked chains read well.
- **Leaving the Warfront costs the wall.** About five minutes in the city took it from 100 to 29 with an
  elite at the gate. The "The wall is cracking." toast warns you, which is good. Five repairers had it back
  to 74 in under a minute.
- **The Minotaur (mini-boss, level 5):** about 85 taps, 35 seconds, 18 renown. A good length.
- **The Breach** (I triggered one by mistake at 20x): the report card is clear: losses in red, the push-back
  and ward in green, one Rebuild button.
- **Chapter 9 (Varn):** the two-person staging works well; the speaker lights up on their side while the
  other dims. Varn then sits in the middle of the wall.

## Problems, most serious first

### 1. Crash: "Maximum update depth exceeded" in PixelBurst

The game closed itself mid-fight. Android's crash log, mapped through the release source map, points at
[PixelBurst.tsx:52](../../src/components/PixelBurst.tsx#L52), the timer that clears a finished strike
effect.

React stops the app when more than 50 updates chain together without a break. A likely cause: while the
screen stalls (Problem 2), strike effects and their cleanup timers pile up, then fire in one burst. A slow
phone with a stalled JS thread could hit the same limit. Needs a closer look and a test; this is a real
crash, not an emulator artifact.

### 2. Freezes: "isn't responding" while pixel art is set up

There were four Android "isn't responding" dialogs. Each time the main thread was waiting for the GPU while
Skia created a drawing surface (`RNSkia::OpenGLWindowContext`, in `SkiaPictureView.surfaceAvailable`).
Every `PixelSprite` and pixel icon is its own Skia `Canvas` with its own GPU surface, so a busy screen, a
scene opening (picture plus busts), or a return to the Warfront creates many at once. The emulator's GPU is
slow at this, so the freezes are worse here; on the A16 it may only show as stutter.

The same cause explains **sprites that pop in late**: twice the fighters (and Varn) were missing from the
wall for several seconds after a sheet closed.

This belongs in the performance pass: count the canvases on the Warfront, and consider drawing the static
ones (the wall's fighters, icons, the HUD) into one shared canvas.

### 3. Things that mislead the player

- **The siege keeps running during story scenes.** A four-line scene is 15 to 20 seconds of monsters
  striking the wall. At 1x it is a few hits; at Dev speeds it caused a Breach. Consider pausing wall strikes
  while a scene is open.
- **Fast taps skip scenes.** My taps on the first kobold went straight through "The Elder Approaches" and
  "Renown". Dev lists both as seen, but I never read them. A player mashing the monster will do the same.
  Ignoring taps for a moment when a scene opens would stop this.
- **A guide highlight in the wrong place.** After a restart, "Open the Forage tab." highlighted the Rally
  price instead of the Forage tab, with the rest of the screen blacked out.
- **Locked tabs don't say how to open them.** Tapping one shows only "Not yet discovered".
  `game-flow.md` says it should show the requirement.
- **The story asks for things already done.** Mirael says "Give me a lodge..." after the map had already
  offered the Lodge and I had built it and promoted two trappers.
- **The quest points to the wrong place.** "Reforge your claws at the Forge", but the reforge button is on
  the claws sheet, not in the Forge.
- **No hint between choices.** With 12 renown, both Rally and the next claws were affordable, and nothing
  hints which helps more now.

### 4. Numbers and words

- **Stray decimals:** "17.00 idle" (Garrison, Lodge, Outpost) and "339.00" food (Outpost). The chip
  formats every value as renown ([ShopScreen.tsx:347](../../src/screens/ShopScreen.tsx#L347)).
- **Forage contradictions:** "Stores last about 20 min" (and 41 min) shows in red, as an alarm, next to a
  green "Well fed: fighters at 110%". The selected Full ration says "hits at 100%" under that 110% badge.
- **Labels that are wrong:**
  - The Forge subtitle reads "1 swords, 1 shields".
  - The Lodge subtitle reads "5/14 rangers" when the five are gatherers.
- **The Outpost:**
  - With no explorer trained, the send button says "Everyone is out".
  - "All out" stayed at 0 while an expedition was out.
- **The night raid toast** ("A night raid struck from silent shadows.") doesn't say what was lost. The food
  quietly dropped by about 20.
- **The welcome-back card** says "Your army could not stop the minotaurs in time" after a 2-minute absence.
  The plural and the blame read oddly for so short a break.

### 5. Look and layout

- **Five button colors for one action.** "Buy this" is red in the Garrison, orange in the Forge, green in
  the Lodge, blue in the Outpost and purple in the Keep. Red also reads as "can't afford" while it is
  affordable. One color for buy, and one for can't afford, would read faster.
- **The scene title covers the counters.** The scene's title ("THE WALL HOLDS") is drawn over the renown
  and food in the top bar.
- **Guide bubbles cover what they explain.** They hide the Garrison's description and the first rows of
  the claws list.
- **The wall's life bar** crosses Varn's head where he perches.
- **"THE DRAGON"** is cramped in its selected Librarium tile.
- **The Orc Warchief** was drawn teal once and gray-orange another time. It may be intended; worth a look.

## Pacing and progression

- **The core loop works.** Kill, earn, buy something that makes the next kill faster, and see it at once.
  The claws are the clearest version of this.
- **Renown per tap is the claw damage times 0.05** (every HP pays 0.05, before studies). Tapping alone
  from Fledgling to Ancient takes about 3,300 taps (Sharpened 100, Hooked 120, Hardened 170, Serrated 250,
  Obsidian 330, Ember 500, Dragonsteel 730, Ancient 1,070). Fighters and soldiers add some, and Rally,
  soldiers, buildings and studies compete for the same renown. Chapter 8 waits on all of it. This is the
  stretch that most needs either more to do or the balance simulator.
- **The early tasks are tap counts, not decisions.** "Gather 200 food by hand" (200 taps) and
  "Rally 5 fighters" (about 400 taps) are both. Halving the first, or giving gatherers to the player sooner,
  would cut the dullest two minutes of the opening.
- **Story pacing (live) can't be judged from this run.** Test pacing chained Keep, Lodge, Ink-Wing,
  They Come Bigger and Beyond the Walls inside two minutes. Worth one live-pacing play on the phone.
- **The city is the emotional payoff.** The fog clearing, the night turning to day at the Keep, and each
  plot appearing are the moments that make you want to keep going. More of that, earlier, would help the
  long middle.

## What to do first

1. Fix the PixelBurst crash (Problem 1), with a test that fires many strikes at once.
2. Pause wall strikes during scenes, and ignore taps for a moment when a scene opens.
3. One buy color across all sheets; whole numbers for people and food.
4. Fix the misleading words: the Outpost button, "1 swords", "5/14 rangers", the reforge quest, the locked
   tabs' requirement.
5. Measure the Skia canvas count on the A16 in the performance pass (Problems 2 and the pop-in).
6. Shorten "Gather 200 food by hand", and plan the middle with the balance simulator.

## The emulator (Player_2), for the record

- **Storage:** it had 378 MB free, too little to install. I used an x86_64-only APK (101 MB) and removed
  the updates to six Google apps (YouTube, YouTube Music, Maps, Gmail, Photos and Docs), which freed about
  1 GB.
- **Host GPU:** two freezes in the GPU pipe, one of them a full emulator crash.
- **Software GPU:** stable longer, but slow (about 6 CPU cores) and it froze once.
- **Last run:** a kernel panic ("Fatal exception in interrupt") restarted Android.
- **Verdict:** usable for short checks of a screen, not for long sessions of this game. Long playtests and
  the performance pass need the A16.

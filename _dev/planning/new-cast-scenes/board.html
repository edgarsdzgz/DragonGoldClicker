The new cast: chapters 9 to 13 (draft v1)

Varn, Sera, Thornwick, Ashara and the Hollow Knight have every face but no lines yet. This is a draft of when each one arrives, what they could bring, and what they say, in the order the old plan had them. The story is paused after chapter 8 (Dragonsteel), so these would be the next five chapters. Each phone plays its chapter over the game's own backdrop, the way the dialogue box shows it: tap (or press Enter) to type a line, tap again for the next. The script beside it lists every line with the face it plays in. The "Brings" line is only a suggestion; what each one does in the game is your decision. Drafted 2026-10-27; nothing here is in the game yet.

Chapter 9

A Small Pair of Wings

When
A minute after the claws are reforged in Dragonsteel (the Reforge the Claws quest). A conversation: two scenes back to back on one stage.
Brings
A second, small dragon on the wall who strikes on his own. Needs a small Varn sprite on the Warfront (new art). Your call.
  1. Lirae the Ink-Wing alarmed

    My lord, someone is circling the Keep. Oh! Oh. Varn? Varn, is that you?

  2. Lirae the Ink-Wing happy

    He was a first-year at the Academy. I thought no one else got out.

  3. Varn happy

    Lirae! And you're the dragon on the gate. You're even bigger than the stories.

  4. Varn grieving

    I hid in the bell tower when the Rift opened. I've been hiding ever since. I'm done hiding.

  5. Varn determined

    I want to fight like you. Let me stand on the wall. I can breathe fire. A little.

Chapter 10

She Did Not Ask

When
During a siege, the next time the wall drops below half its strength after Varn's chapter.
Brings
A shield wall: monsters that reach the wall strike it for less. Your call.
Sting
Sera (shield bash), as they first appear
  1. Sera the Shieldmaiden determined

    Shields up, little ones! Hold that line!

  2. Sera the Shieldmaiden happy

    Hah! That's better. So you're the gate-dragon. Sera, of the northern clans.

  3. Sera the Shieldmaiden grieving

    My clan held the high pass until there was no pass left to hold. I walked south, toward the sound of a fight.

  4. Sera the Shieldmaiden neutral

    Don't thank me. Give me the front of the wall and a shield that won't split.

Chapter 11

The Exile

When
An expedition brings him home: the first expedition to return after Sera's chapter. A conversation: two scenes back to back on one stage.
Brings
The Wizard's Tower, as the story plan already says (its interior header is drawn). Your call.
Sting
Thornwick (spark), as they first appear
  1. Scout Mirael worried

    My lord, the expedition found someone in the eastern ruins. He was setting a monster on fire at the time.

  2. Scout Mirael neutral

    He says he is a mage. He also says the fire was on purpose.

  3. Thornwick happy

    Thornwick, at your service. Formerly of the eastern towers. They asked me to leave after the third fire.

  4. Thornwick alarmed

    The first two were not my fault! The third was... a learning experience.

  5. Thornwick determined

    Give me a tower, high up and well away from anything that burns, and I will show that horde what a real fire looks like.

Chapter 12

The Eldest

When
The first time the Unmaking rises to its highest tier. A conversation: two scenes back to back on one stage.
Brings
Her strength behind yours: your strikes hit harder. Your call.
  1. Elder Pyreth alarmed

    My lord, something vast is coming down from the mountain. No, hold your fire!

  2. Elder Pyreth happy

    Ashara. I thought we had lost you. She is the eldest of us, my lord, older than these walls.

  3. Ashara the Elder Wyrm neutral

    Pyreth. You got old. And this is the one who holds the gate? Smaller than I pictured.

  4. Ashara the Elder Wyrm grieving

    I slept beneath the mountain while the world came apart. I woke to silence where my kin should be.

  5. Ashara the Elder Wyrm angry

    I am old. I am tired. And I am very, very angry.

  6. Ashara the Elder Wyrm determined

    Let them come to the wall. They will learn why the old ones were feared.
